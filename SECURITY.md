# Security policy

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to the address in
[`apps/web/public/.well-known/security.txt`](apps/web/public/.well-known/security.txt)
(replace the placeholder with your real contact before publishing). Please do not open a public
issue. Include steps to reproduce; we aim to acknowledge within three working days.

Scope: this repository's web app and API. Out of scope: the simulated sellers and commitments (no
real money moves), denial of service by volume, and social engineering of maintainers.

How the app defends itself, what was audited, and what is still open is in
[`docs/security.md`](docs/security.md).
